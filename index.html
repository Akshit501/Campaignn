Everything You Need to Know

Got questions about participating or partnering? Find instant answers below.

Who is eligible to participate in the hackathon? +
The hackathon is open to undergraduate and graduate students, professional software developers, data scientists, AI researchers, and early-stage startup teams across India. Working professionals and collegiate developers may form teams together.
Is registration completely free for participants? +
Yes! 100% free. There is zero registration fee. Selected finalists invited to the offline Grand Finale in Bengaluru receive complimentary meals, official ET swag, GPU compute credits, and travel stipends for shortlisted intercity teams.
What is the permitted team size? +
Teams can consist of 1 to 4 members. We strongly encourage interdisciplinary teams combining deep AI/ML engineers with full-stack developers and product/domain thinkers.
Who owns the Intellectual Property (IP) of the software built? +
Participants retain 100% ownership of their intellectual property. Neither The Economic Times nor hackathon sponsors claim ownership over your code, unless you mutually enter into a formal commercial pilot agreement or acquisition with a track sponsor following the event.
How can my enterprise sponsor or co-create a challenge track? +
Enterprises can join as a Title Partner, Track Innovation Partner, Platform & Cloud Partner or Associate Partner. Fill in the partnership enquiry form or contact our partnerships desk at hackathon@economictimes.com. We provide full co-branded integration, custom problem statement definition, and media campaigns across ET assets.
How much does sponsorship cost? +
Pricing depends on the tier and the deliverables you choose. Request the sponsor deck through the enquiry form for current tier pricing, or book a call with the partnerships team to build a custom package.
What hardware, compute, and API keys are provided? +
All shortlisted Phase 2 and Phase 3 teams receive subsidized or fully sponsored API credits for leading LLMs and specialized cloud GPU clusters provided by our cloud hyperscaler partners.
